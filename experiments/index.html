Experiments

Research claims will be associated with the evidence that supports them. A simulation is not presented as a physical experiment, and a computational result is not presented as independent validation unless such validation has actually been performed.

Evidence discipline: an empty experiment registry means that no specific experiment record has been published here yet. Records will be added as studies are documented.
